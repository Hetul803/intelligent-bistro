# Loom Demo Script

1. Open with: “This is Intelligent Bistro, a futuristic AI-powered restaurant ordering experience.”
2. Show that the app opens on AI chat, not the menu.
3. Tap “Reset Loom Demo” so the walkthrough starts clean.
4. Use: “Build dinner for two under 900 calories each.” Show calorie-aware planning and the AI decision card.
5. Open the Structured JSON panel and show validated actions.
6. Use “pair a drink” or “remove sauce” to show chat-based cart edits.
7. Approve the order and show receipt calories plus the kitchen timeline.
8. Cancel the order, then use Reorder to Cart.
9. Briefly show backend route, Zod schema, local planner, optional Ollama path, and optional OpenAI Structured Outputs path.
10. Show `/prompts` and `/docs/architecture.md` to explain AI-assisted workflow and system design.
