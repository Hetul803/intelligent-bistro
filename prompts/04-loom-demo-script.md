1. Open with: “This is Intelligent Bistro, a futuristic AI-powered restaurant ordering experience.”
2. Show that the app opens on AI chat, not the menu.
3. Use: “I want something light or healthy.” Show that the AI narrows the menu to two choices instead of making the user browse.
4. Use: “healthy and filling.” Show the Veggie Power Bowl added and the drink follow-up question.
5. Use: “I need something spicy and under 10 dollars.” Show that the AI refuses the impossible match and displays the closest option.
6. Use: “I need something spicy but under 20 dollars.” Show the Spicy Chicken Sandwich added and the drink follow-up question.
7. Click “Plan group” and show vegetarian, mild, budget, and drink guardrails.
8. Open the JSON panel and show validated structured actions.
9. Briefly show backend route, Zod schema, OpenAI service path, and deterministic fallback parser.
10. Show `/prompts` folder and explain AI-assisted workflow.
