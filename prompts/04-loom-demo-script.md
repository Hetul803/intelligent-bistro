# Loom Demo Script

1. Open with: “This is Intelligent Bistro, a futuristic AI-powered restaurant ordering experience.”
2. Show that the app opens on AI chat, not the menu.
3. Use: “I need something spicy and under 10 dollars.” Show that the AI refuses the impossible match and displays the closest option.
4. Use: “I need something spicy but under 20 dollars.” Show the Spicy Chicken Sandwich added and the drink follow-up question.
5. Use: “clear the entire order” and “remove all the items.”
6. Click “Plan group” and show vegetarian, mild, budget, and drink guardrails.
7. Open the JSON panel and show validated structured actions.
8. Toggle to the manual Menu view, then return to AI.
9. Briefly show backend route, Zod schema, OpenAI service path, and deterministic fallback parser.
10. Show `/prompts` folder and explain AI-assisted workflow.
